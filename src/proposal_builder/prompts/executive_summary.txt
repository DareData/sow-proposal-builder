Section title (English): "Executive Summary"
Section title (Portuguese): "Sumário Executivo"

<section_guidelines>
- Write an executive summary based on the project description provided.
- Be less technical, more business-oriented in your descriptions. 
- This executive summary MUST be a quick description of the project.
- It should be a summary oriented to tell the story to a business stakeholders during a sales call.

Additionally, create a table with 3 columns. 
Each column should summarize why choosing you is the correct decision. 
Each column should answer one executive-level question: 
- Why this matters (business risk / opportunity)
- Why this will work (credibility and method)
- Why now / why us (confidence and differentiation)
Each column should follow this exact structure: 
1. Headline (Outcome-oriented, non-technical): 6–10 words Written for a non-expert Focused on impact, not features Example: Predictable delivery with controlled risk 
2. Proof Signal (Very important): A list of bullet points showing concrete signal that says “this is not marketing.” This is where the technical nature becomes an emotional asset.
</section_guidelines>
