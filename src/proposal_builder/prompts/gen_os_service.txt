Service is the AI solution for customer conversations across service channels. The agent answers questions, resolves enquiries and escalates to a human when confidence levels drop or the conversation requires it.

Unlike generic chatbots that can be set up in a matter of days but fail without any control, Service is built for channels where an incorrect response has real-world consequences: compliance, reputation, refunds. Every response is based on approved, cited and auditable sources.

Capabilities in production:

Disciplined retrieval. The agent only responds when it finds the information in the knowledge base. No retrieval, no response. It does not make things up.
Cited responses. Each response includes the source and the original passage, traceable by compliance and audit teams.
Action execution. Check order status, change address, cancel subscription – all within the conversation, linked to the client’s systems.
Memory. Continuity between sessions for returning customers.
On the GenOS platform, the operations team has full visibility: analytics on conversations and escalations, recurring topics, human quality review, knowledge audits to identify gaps, and automated tests to prevent regressions.

The Service is currently deployed via an embeddable chat widget. Voice, WhatsApp and email are on the roadmap as future channels.
