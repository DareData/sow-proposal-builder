The Gen‑OS platform — an enterprise-grade operations framework designed to make GenAI solutions scalable, safe, and sustainable over time. Gen‑OS goes beyond one-off tooling by embedding governance, observability, and continuous improvement directly into the solution stack.

Rather than placing the burden of operations on internal teams, Gen‑OS delivers a ready-to-use infrastructure for managing agents at scale — across business units, use cases, and deployment environments. It ensures consistent quality, auditability, and agility by providing a unified control layer for monitoring, escalation, and iteration.

For business users, this means GenAI solutions that are trustworthy and continuously optimised without requiring technical overhead. For IT and innovation teams, it unlocks a modular platform to manage multiple agents under one roof — with centralised governance, usage visibility, and version control.

In contrast to isolated agent builds, Gen‑OS supports limitless horizontal scaling by allowing new use cases to plug into the same operational backbone — reducing total cost of ownership and accelerating time-to-value, without vendor lock-in.

Gen-OS offers three core components:

#### 1. Applications

Stable, opinionated, and self-serve products to support the workforce. AI embedded in real workflows — not side experiments.

- **Assistant:** A chat interface that serves as a centralised system for all conversational agents. A workforce tool to improve individual and team productivity with RAG, web search, individual agent creation, file upload, collaboration, and memory.
- **Service:** An omni-channel tool to support all customer service operations. Agents that connect with existing omni-channel systems to automate customer service enquiries and monitor performance across those agents.
- **Supervisor:** A dedicated tool for human-in-the-loop case management, designed to enhance workflow automations with human intervention, observability, compliance, and cross-team collaboration. Failed or sensitive cases can be escalated for human review, validated, or routed to the appropriate support teams — ensuring safe and compliant decision-making in agent-driven processes.

#### 2. Intelligence

Agents engineered to connect with real systems, maintained and improved over time. Out-of-the-box templates for fast, curated operations with full integration across the Gen-OS ecosystem (Applications and Platform). This includes agents, tools, workflows, and connectors to the most common ERPs and CRMs, with custom integrations available in the customisation phase.

#### 3. Platform

The Gen-OS platform serves as the central hub for governance, monitoring, auditability, human oversight, and continuous improvement. Key features include:

- **Monitoring:** Observability of generative workflows — automatic resolution rates, human intervention per workflow step, FinOps tracking, and more. For conversational agents: historical topic detection, sentiment analysis by topic, usage and cost analytics.
- **Improvement:** A continuous improvement engine that analyses agent failure patterns, proposes updates, and enables safe testing of new logic or configurations. Triggered manually via collaborator issue tickets or automatically through content audits (knowledge gap detection based on content and conversation history), evaluation set generation, and more.
