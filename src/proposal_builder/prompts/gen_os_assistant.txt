The Assistant is the workspace where employees interact with AI, company knowledge and internal systems in a secure, transparent and controlled manner.

Unlike generic assistants built for general knowledge tasks, the Assistant is designed to operate within the company’s actual operations: connected to the right information sources, with appropriate permissions, and with a cycle of continuous improvement based on real-world usage.

Three agent configurations cater to different needs, with no hierarchy between them:

Personal Agent. Individual productivity from day one. Document analysis, calculations, web searches, summaries, drafts. No company configuration is required to get started.
Company Agents. Linked to the organisation’s shared knowledge (policies, procedures, internal documentation). Any employee can ask a question and receive cited, traceable answers. Common configurations: HR, Finance, IT.
Team Agents. Specialised for a specific role or workflow, with deep access to operational systems (CRM, ERP, internal APIs). Common configurations: Sales, Laboratory, Operations, Support.

All agents share the same core capabilities: document processing (PDF, Excel, images, OCR), code execution, web search, cross-session memory, and integration with business tools. What sets them apart is their scope: who they serve and what knowledge and systems they access.

The Assistant runs on GenOS, the platform that ensures governance, monitoring and continuous improvement of the entire AI operation.
